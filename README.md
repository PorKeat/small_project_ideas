## 💡 Quick Overview of Each Project

### 1. [ChamNeak (ចាំអ្នក)](./project-ideas/01-chamneak/README.md)
* **What it does:** Replaces paper stamp cards with zero-app QR scans for cafes and online shops.
* **Why it matters:** Customers never lose stamps, and owners can trigger automated 30-day "win-back" messages.

### 2. [Minuy (មីនុយ)](./project-ideas/02-minuy/README.md)
* **What it does:** Instant QR table menu with real-time ordering sent straight to staff Telegram groups.
* **Why it matters:** Eliminates paper menu reprinting costs and speeds up cafe/restaurant table turnover.

### 3. [Phtieng (ផ្ទៀង)](./project-ideas/03-phtieng/README.md)
* **What it does:** Automated OCR payment slip verifier & address collection bot for Facebook Live & Telegram sellers.
* **Why it matters:** Stops fake bank transfer slip fraud and saves sellers 3–4 hours of manual chat checking every night.

### 4. [Ven (វេន)](./project-ideas/04-ven/README.md)
* **What it does:** Smart digital queue and appointment booking via Telegram for barbers, salons, and clinics.
* **Why it matters:** Eliminates crowded waiting lobbies and notifies clients 5 minutes before their turn.

### 5. [KitLuy (គិតលុយ)](./project-ideas/05-kitluy/README.md)
* **What it does:** 30-second mobile invoicing with embedded Bakong/ABA KHQR codes and daily cash tracking.
* **Why it matters:** Replaces messy handwritten receipts with professional branded PDF bills.

---

## 🎯 Core Philosophy
1. **Zero Customer Friction:** No mandatory app downloads — runs via instant web passes, QR codes, or existing chat apps (Telegram, Messenger).
2. **Accessible Flat Pricing:** Predictable, ultra-affordable subscriptions (**$3–$12/month**) designed for micro-businesses.
3. **No Heavy Hardware:** Everything operates on smartphones, tablets, or shared chat channels.

## 💡 About the Name
**KitLuy (គិតលុយ)** literally translates to **"calculate money / settle the bill"** in Khmer. It is the universal phrase spoken across all Cambodian shops, cafes, and restaurants when asking for the bill.

---

## 🛑 The Problem
1. **Unprofessional Hand-Written Receipts:** Small service providers and freelancers send messy text messages or paper slips.
2. **Accounting Software Overkill:** Tools like QuickBooks or Xero are too expensive ($30+/mo) and far too complex for micro-businesses.

## 🔑 Key Features
* **Create in 30 Seconds:** Enter client name, item, and price on your phone.
* **Auto-Generate Payment QR:** System embeds your dynamic Bakong/ABA KHQR code directly onto the invoice.
* **Share via Telegram / Messenger:** Send an elegant, branded PDF or image invoice straight to the client.
* **Track Daily Income:** Real-time summary of paid vs unpaid invoices.
